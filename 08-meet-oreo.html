Meet Oreo

I named him Oreo because, well — he looked like the actual cookie. White fur right down the middle, black on both sides, like the cream getting squished out in the center. It just fit.

He's a purebred Shih Tzu, born in 2021, and he's turning five this September 12. As a puppy he was hyper — running around every room in the house like the floor was on fire. My family had never had a dog before, and honestly, we were terrified of dogs in general. So when this tiny, loud, non-stop ball of energy showed up, the whole house went into chaos. I was scared of him too, if I'm being honest.

But somehow I became the one who was with him at night — making sure he didn't pee everywhere, trying to calm him down when he wouldn't stop barking. We were like cats and dogs, literally and figuratively. Whenever I tried to catch him, he'd turn it around and chase me instead, so little me would just run — except now I was the one running away. Somewhere in all that back-and-forth, he stopped being "the dog we were scared of" and just became Oreo.
